The History of Independence Day In India

Independence Day, in India, is a national holiday celebrated annually on August 15. Independence Day marks the end of the British Raj in 1947, brought about by the Indian Independence Act of July 18 that year, and the establishment of a free and independent Indian nation following the long struggle of the Indian Independence Movement. It also marks the anniversary of the partition of India into two countries, India and Pakistan, which occurred at midnight on August 14/15, 1947. India celebrates Independence Day on August 15, whereas Pakistan observes it on August 14, in recognition of the date on which independence was formally declared there.
